Dream Dust is the first formula most keepers brew, and the one that opens the game's automation door: it is a quest item for **Sweet Dreams**, the quest that grants your first zombies. It is also one of the three ingredients for summoning the Fairy. Everything below is checked to **October 24, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Runes needed | **1 green, 1 blue** (exact match) |
| Laboratory | Laboratory I or II |
| Working mixes | 23 documented combinations |
| Unlocked by | **Alchemy, Basic** technology |
| Sells for | 68–72 bronze (Alchemist, at usual stock) |
| Quest uses | Sweet Dreams · Fairy summoning |

## Cheapest mixes

Twenty two-ingredient combinations work in total — any green carrier plus any blue carrier. These are the nine cheapest documented pairs, ordered by combined ingredient price:

| Mix |
|---|
| Berry + Blueberry |
| Berry + Blood |
| Seed oil + Berry |
| Leaf + Blueberry |
| Water + Blueberry |
| Leaf + Blood |
| Water + Blood |
| Seed oil + Leaf |
| Water + Seed oil |

The remaining pairs rely on rarer blue carriers. They work, but they are not worth shopping for when berries and leaves grow on your doorstep.

Practical picks, by how easy they are to get:

- **Leaf + Blueberry** — leaves drop two at a time from every garden bed harvest; blueberries come from blueberry bushes (2–4 per bush).
- **Berry + Blood** — ashberry bushes for the berry, any autopsy for the blood.
- **Water + Seed oil** — the well is free and infinite; seed oil comes from the millstone (4 seeds of flax, wheat, beet, carrot or cabbage).

## How to brew it

1. **Study the ingredients** you plan to use — the lab rejects un-studied materials.
2. **Mix exactly 1 green and 1 blue.** The lab sums colour values; two greens or two blues will not work.
3. **Farm it early.** Alchemy, Basic is a low technology rung, so Dream Dust is usually your first brewed formula.

If you would rather not build the lab yet, the Alchemist sells finished Dream Dust for 68–72 bronze — the honest shortcut before Laboratory I exists.

## Where the two quests take it

- **Sweet Dreams** (Plague Doctor) — brew 1 Dream Dust, then create your first two zombies. This is the gate to the entire [zombie workforce](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/).
- **Chasing Fairy Lights** — the fairy summoning takes **1 Dream Dust, 1 Comet's Tail and 1 Tooth**, and needs Farming mastery 3. The reward is the Fairy's Swamp Map, which the [main story](/quests/main-quest-walkthrough/) needs for the swamp route.

Hold one extra unit before the zombies quest chain starts: the fairy recipe comes up soon after, and a second trip to the lab is avoidable.
