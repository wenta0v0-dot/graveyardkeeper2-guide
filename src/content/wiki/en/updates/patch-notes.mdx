Graveyard Keeper 2 is being patched fast — seven updates in the first three weeks after the September 22 launch — and the patch notes matter more than usual, because several of them changed the economy and killed progression-blocking bugs. This page condenses every update into a dated log, sourced from the official announcement feed. Last checked **October 17, 2026**; this page is re-verified after every patch, the same promise the [codes page](/codes/all-codes/) makes.

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  Version trail: launch hotfix (Sep 22) → **1.005** (Sep 24) → **1.006** (Sep 25) → **1.007** (Sep 28) → **1.008** (Oct 2) → **1.009** (Oct 6) → **1.010** (Oct 13). If you stalled on a progression bug before 1.008, try again — the main-quest softlock and the worst crashers were fixed. The delivery economy got buffed in 1.008, and order slots grew to 5 in 1.010.

## What each update actually changed

### Update 1.005 — the morgue and stations

- Fixed the **Study Table locking up** after inserting Science — the freeze that looked like a broken save.
- Fixed prepared bodies and strapped corpses resetting state, corpses stuck in the Crematorium, and zombies **failing to resurrect**.
- Rebalanced sawing output (sawing gives more logs) and the Kiln/Furnace split.
- Added the missing zombie-limit hint on PlayStation.

### Update 1.006 — bug reporting and the economy

- **In-game bug reporting** on Steam PC.
- Winning fights now earns **red tech points** (previously wasted effort).
- Fixed **fixed-price trade goods duplicating** on the first purchase of a session.
- **Demo-save milestones now unlock Steam achievements retroactively** — the biggest quality-of-life news for anyone who played the September demo.
- Defender's Emblems can no longer be destroyed (this protects your zombie squad unlocks), plus ultrawide and crop-bed fixes.

### Changelog 1.007 — crashes and duplication

- A wave of crash fixes: repeated exception popups, picking up the zombie caretaker, characters wedged in scenery.
- **Zombie fights now earn red tech points** for the player.
- Duplication and exploit fixes: bag-to-bag copying, alchemy duplication, and the dynamite destroy-and-sell exploit.
- Fixed the **~2 FPS freeze while saving**.
- Fixed a quest translation blocking a quest, the PR machine, iron-supply delivery quests, and self-study books.

### Update 1.008 — the delivery economy rebalance

This is the patch that changed how money works:

- **Fewer crates needed per delivery** and **more likes from deliveries** — the [factory supply chain](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) got meaningfully more profitable.
- **More likes from vendor trading** and a larger **Happy Supplier** perk bonus.
- **More Town Gratitude from winning battles.**
- Fixed: the going-to-bed crash, the **infinite loading screen** (conveyor exception), Town Warehouse and vine-cellar collisions, fast travel during battle, and a **main-quest softlock** — see the [walkthrough's save-before-the-choice warning](/quests/main-quest-walkthrough/) for the spot that still deserves a hard save.

### Changelog 1.009 — crashes, HUD locks and zombie stations

- Fixed a **crash while loading a save** and the player or HUD staying **locked after returning to the main menu** or starting a new game.
- Fixed the **day not rolling over** when the clock landed exactly on the end of the day — it showed up as a day jump in the quests UI after a sermon.
- Fixed the **nun's base upgrade softlock**, plus collisions around conveyor storage, the crematorium and the resurrection table.
- Zombie stations: sawmill, mine, clay pit and sand pit workers no longer get stuck after carrying results back; caretakers can be picked up or inspected mid-shift; a gardener whose harvest keeps failing returns to its station and leaves the crop for you.
- Existing-save corrections: the pre-order **Sauna debuff** no longer permanently reduces energy food restores, and the tier-2 oven stays hidden from the tech tree until it is repaired.
- This version also rolled out to **PlayStation and Xbox**.

### Update 1.010 — the softlock sweep and bigger order books

- **Order slots now rise up to 5** depending on villager count — a direct buff to the [town order income](/guides/money-guide/).
- **Belt Master perk** zombies give more gears, and the zombie porter no longer walks through factory walls.
- A roughly twenty-fix softlock sweep: stuck behind the bed after loading, the dungeon from the Town Warehouse, the sewer fight, crypt building, falling through the Floating Village, and more.
- Fixed the consistent **crash in Battle Operation Potato Part I** — the route the Act 6 Envy fragment runs through.
- Fixed inspirations requiring more actions than the world allows, the NPC "..." chatter bug, and several controller issues.

## What the developers have planned

The official roadmap post (undated items) lists: **zombie helpers**, **additional order slots**, **trader upgrades**, **Panic Reduction**, **rechargeable Hypno-devices**, and **zombie cremation**. No dates, no multiplayer or co-op announcement.
