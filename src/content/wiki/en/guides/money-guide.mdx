Most Graveyard Keeper 2 money advice is a list of chores. That advice is dated the moment your energy bar becomes the bottleneck — which happens fast. The question is not "what can I sell" but "what keeps selling when I am asleep". This guide ranks the streams that scale. Checked against community documentation current to **version 1.009** (October 17, 2026); where prices could not be confirmed, the guide says so instead of inventing numbers.

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  Four streams scale: **crops** (wheat, carrots, beets → village traders) to start, the **paint chain** as your first true production line, **town orders on purple days** for coin and Town Gratitude, and **church income** running on rails in the background. Everything else is busywork your zombie workforce should be doing — see the [zombie workers guide](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/).
</Callout>

## What earns money in the early game?

Crops, because the setup cost is nearly zero. Wheat, carrots and beets all sell to village traders, and the farm rows feed themselves once a zombie gardener works them. This is the stream that pays while you are still fighting the graveyard and tech-tree battles described in the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).

The rule that carries through the whole game: **raw goods never out-earn what you craft from them**. Sell the harvest, but keep the first processed line in your sights.

## The paint production chain

The community's favorite early production line, because the final step costs no energy:

1. Techs: **Garden Improvement** (tier 1), **Glass Blowing**, **Paint from Anatomy and Alchemy**.
2. Inputs: **water from the Garden Well**, **sand** (the bridge by the yard), **firewood**.
3. Glass from sand and firewood feeds the alchemy flasks.
4. **Study water** to learn the green rune.
5. Combine **2 Water → Paint** — the combination itself costs no energy.

The port trader buys paint. It is a repeatable, low-touch loop, and it is the template for every production line after it: cheap inputs, one study unlock, a buyer who never says no.

## Merchant orders and the town

Repeatable town orders land on **purple days** of the six-day calendar. They pay **Town Gratitude** and sometimes coin directly — and gratitude is itself an investment, because it opens more of the town, more traders, and more orders. The orders and the warehouse have their own quirks (Jack included), which the [troubleshooting guide](/guides/troubleshooting/) covers in detail.

## Church income

The graveyard converts corpses into quality, quality converts into sermons, sermons convert into steady faith and coin. It is the income stream that runs on rails — build it early via the church unlock, keep the graveyard quality growing, and it pays for the rest of the map. The full loop is in the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/); the faith side of it is in the [faith guide](/guides/faith-guide/).

## What only looks like profit

Three traps eat evenings for pocket change:

- **Hand-hauling anything a porter zombie could carry.** Your energy is the scarcest currency in the game; spending it on logistics is a loss.
- **Crafting without a buyer in mind.** A full chest is not profit; it is storage you now have to empty.
- **Grinding raw materials for sale.** The factory and alcohol lines exist precisely because processed goods out-earn raw ones around the clock.
