Graveyard Keeper 2 Fishing — All 22 Fish, Bait and Spots

Every Graveyard Keeper 2 fish with its bait, spot, best time, fight difficulty and price — plus the four legendary catches no trader will buy.

10/27/2026 1.010 GK2 Guide Last updated: 10/27/2026 5 min read

Quick Answer

Graveyard Keeper 2 has 22 fish across six spots — Inquisition Base, Village, Forest, Port Area, Swamp and Sewers — and four baits: none, Breadcrumb, Insect and Live. This page lists every fish with bait, spot, best time, fight difficulty and buyer, plus the four legendary catches no trader buys.

FAQ

How many fish are in Graveyard Keeper 2?
22 species across six fishing spots. Four are legendary catches no trader will buy: Mythic Carp, Kraken Jr, Celestial Salmon and Elder Catfish.
What baits exist?
Four: no bait, Breadcrumb Bait, Insect Bait and Live Bait. Higher-tier fish need better bait — Live Bait is reserved for the four legendary catches.
What does fight difficulty mean?
It is the number you must beat during the catch. The Fisherman perk roughly cuts it by a third — for example the Elder Catfish drops from 20 to 14.
Which spot has the best fish?
Port Area and Forest for sea fish, Swamp for the exotic catches, Village for river fish. The Sewers only add Eel to the Swamp list.

What to do with the catch

  • Sell the river and sea fish. Roach through Trout all have buyers; that is the reliable coin line for the early money guide.
  • The four legendary fish are not for sale. Each turns into a signature dish instead: Kraken steak, Heavenly salmon, Elder’s meal and Fried golden carp. They are the reason to fish with Live Bait at all.
  • Fillets feed cooking. River fish give river fillets, sea fish give sea fillets, and the mysterious catches give their own cut — used in Fish nuggets, Baked fish, Fish soup, Baked salmon and Fisherman’s pie.
  • Frog is the odd one out. It bites on no bait, Breadcrumb and Insect, which makes it the easiest unusual catch in the Swamp.

Practical fishing plan

  1. No bait first — build kitchen stock from Roach and Gudgeon while your fishing mastery grows.
  2. Breadcrumb Bait unlocks the money fish: Mackerel, Bream, Catfish, Silverfin.
  3. Insect Bait is where the value is — Eel at 1s 92b is the best repeatable catch in the game.
  4. Live Bait only when hunting legendaries — the fight values (17–20) are brutal without the Fisherman perk.
  5. Fish at night for Crucian, Mackerel, Carp, Salmon and Swamp Ghost; that is where the “best by night” lines pay off.

Community data records a 99.3% bite chance at the Inquisition Base with no bait at night — the safest place to practise the timing minigame before spending bait.
