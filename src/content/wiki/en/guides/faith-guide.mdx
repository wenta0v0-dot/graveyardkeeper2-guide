Faith is the resource Graveyard Keeper 2 players run out of first, because the sequel turned it from a sermon currency into a backbone resource — it buys Inspiration, it is part of creating zombies, and it even gates some fast travel. That makes every point of weekly income matter more than it did in the first game. Checked against community documentation current to **version 1.009** (October 17, 2026).

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  Income side: open the **church** via the Nun's quest line, then preach **weekly** — prayer quality and **Town Reputation** shape the yield. Spending side: faith feeds the **Inspiration bar** and goes into **zombie creation**. Spend on the workforce even when it hurts — a maxed zombie crew is the point where faith stops being the bottleneck.
</Callout>

## What is faith actually for?

Three documented sinks, and they pull in different directions:

- **The Inspiration bar** — faith feeds it, and Inspiration gates the work that makes everything else faster. The community starter advice is blunt: spend it wisely, because it is doing more jobs than it used to.
- **Zombie creation** — making workers costs faith, which makes the [zombie workers guide](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/) and this page the same economy read from two ends.
- **Fast travel** — some travel points cost faith to use. Convenient, but it is the same wallet.

## How do you earn faith?

The engine is the church, and the church is a quest line away:

1. **Unlock the church through the Nun's quest line.** Until it is open, your faith income is effectively zero — which is why the church belongs in your first-week plan (see the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/)).
2. **Preach weekly.** Sermons are the heartbeat; missing a week is missing income you do not get back.
3. **Mind the two multipliers.** Prayer quality and **Town Reputation** both shape what a sermon is worth. Graveyard quality feeds reputation, so the burial habits you keep are quietly a faith strategy too.
4. **Choose prayers deliberately.** The community has documented **26 prayers and 58 sermons** with unlock conditions and costs — an external reference worth bookmarking while we build our own verified table.

## Why does faith feel so slow?

Because early on it is: small sermons, low reputation, and three sinks tugging at the same wallet. Multiple community threads land on the same answer, and it is counterintuitive — **do not hoard faith to avoid the pain**. The zombie workforce is a faith investment that pays itself back by making every other system passive; at a full crew, the bottleneck moves somewhere else entirely. Hoarded faith, meanwhile, is just unspent income.

## Spending priorities

Ranked by how the community plays it, cheapest long-term value first:

1. **The workforce.** Every collar is energy you stop spending and stations that run while you sleep.
2. **Inspiration, when it gates the next unlock.** The tech tree is the game's longest pole (the [troubleshooting guide](/guides/troubleshooting/) covers the worst wall on it), and Inspiration is one of the levers that moves it.
3. **Fast travel, sparingly.** It is a convenience, not an investment — pay it from surplus, not from the workforce budget.
