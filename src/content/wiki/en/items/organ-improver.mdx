Organ Improver is the formula that turns corpses from a chore into a resource. It is also the most confusing entry on the alchemy list, because **two different formulas share one name** and the game does not spell out the difference. Here it is, plainly. Checked **October 24, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Red-skull version | **4 red, 1 green** |
| White-skull version | **4 green** |
| Laboratory | Laboratory I or II |
| Working mixes | 333 documented combinations (both versions) |
| Unlocked by | **Practical Potions** technology |
| Used at | the **Formulation Bench** |

## Which version do you want?

| If you want… | Brew | Because |
|---|---|---|
| A corpse with high **red** skulls (better zombie worker) | 4 red + 1 green | Converts an organ to gold with **3 red skulls** |
| A corpse with high **white** skulls (better burial value) | 4 green | Converts an organ to gold with **3 white skulls** |

Each conversion consumes **one Organ Improver** and acts on a single organ, so count your organs before you brew a batch.

## Cheapest mixes

The documented cheapest mixes split cleanly by version:

**Red-skull version (4 red, 1 green)**

| Mix | Rune math |
|---|---|
| **Copper Nugget + Red Mushroom** | 2R 1G + 2R |
| Stone + Copper Nugget | 2R + 2R 1G |
| Tooth + Melted Fat | 1R 1G + 3R |

**White-skull version (4 green)**

| Mix | Rune math |
|---|---|
| **Swamp mushroom + Swamp mushroom** | 2G + 2G |
| Flour + Swamp mushroom | 2G + 2G |
| Flour + Flour | 2G + 2G |

The practical pair: **Copper Nugget + Red Mushroom** for the worker version and **Flour + Flour** for the burial version. Both inputs are renewable — copper from Copper Veins (pickaxe, Smithing 2), red mushrooms from nodes, flour from the Millstone (4 Wheat).

## Where the ingredients come from

- **Copper Nugget** — Copper Vein, pickaxe, Smithing 2.
- **Red Mushroom** — red mushroom nodes, 2–3 per node.
- **Stone** — stone sources and junk piles, or a zombie stone quarry.
- **Tooth** — Formulation Bench: any skull (Skull and Teeth tech).
- **Melted Fat** — Apothecary, or the Distillation Cube (Fat / 3 Seed oil + 2 Clay Plates).
- **Swamp mushroom** — green mushroom nodes, 2–3 per node.
- **Flour** — Millstone: 4 Wheat; also sold by the Baker.

Every value above is in the [alchemy cheat sheet](/items/alchemy-runes-cheat-sheet/) with the rest of the table.

## How it fits the rest of the game

- **Burial quality** — the white-skull version feeds graveyard rating, which the [achievement list](/achievements/all-achievements/) tracks up to quality 200.
- **Zombie throughput** — the red-skull version feeds corpse quality, and corpse quality is what lets a worker run a station without you. See the [zombie workers guide](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/).
- **Faith** — better corpses also mean better sermons; the [faith guide](/guides/faith-guide/) explains why quality compounds.
