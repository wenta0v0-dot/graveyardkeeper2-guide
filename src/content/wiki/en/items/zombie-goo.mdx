Zombie Goo is the easiest blue rune in the game to end up short of: it is a battle reward, not a farm, and ten formulas want it. The good news is that battles you have to fight anyway pay six at a time. Checked **October 27, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Buys for | **1 silver 50 bronze** (Gunter) |
| Sells for | 1 silver 7 bronze |
| Stack | 99 |
| Study | 5 tech points |
| Alchemy rune | **2 blue** (10 formulas) |
| Best source | Battle wins — 6 each |

## Where to get Zombie Goo

**1. Battles (main source)** — a **6-per-win** reward across **24 battles**, starting with *Break Through to the Port* and including *Eastern Districts: Active Scouting*. Since town battles recur on Sloth days, this is also the renewable tap.

**2. Gunter (buy)** — 1 silver 50 bronze. Reasonable when one brew is blocked; expensive as a habit, given the 99 stack and how often you win battles anyway.

There is no zombie station and no crafting recipe, which surprises most players the first time they look.

## What Zombie Goo is for

Ten alchemy formulas use it, all as a pure blue carrier:

| Formula | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Reanimation Liquid** | every zombie revival |
| Zombie Motivator Potion II / III | zombie fighters attack faster |
| Berserker Potion III | no stamina cost on attacks |
| Third Eye Salt, Umami, Alkali | high-tier formulas |
| Spices, Explosive Dust, Lacquer | mid-tier formulas |

Full rune math and the cheapest mixes are in the [alchemy cheat sheet](/items/alchemy-runes-cheat-sheet/) and on the [Reanimation Liquid](/items/reanimation-liquid/) page.

## How to keep a stock

- **Bank the story battles.** The main quest forces most of the 24 battles; by Act 5 you should have a healthy pile without trying.
- **Farm Sloth-day town battles** for top-ups — see the weekday locks in the [walkthrough](/quests/main-quest-walkthrough/).
- **Do not sell it.** At 1 silver 7 bronze the sale value is trivial against what one Reanimation Liquid is worth.
