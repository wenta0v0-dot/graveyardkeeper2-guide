Every tool in Graveyard Keeper 2 follows one ladder, and knowing it saves real money: **Rusty +1, Bronze +2, Iron +4, Steel +6** mastery while in the tool belt. The same recipe shape repeats — 2 Stick plus two details plus one ingot — so tool upgrades are really an ingot budget question. Checked **October 27, 2026** (version 1.010).

## The tier ladder

| Tier | Mastery in belt | Typical price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rusty | +1 | 10 bronze | Prologue gifts or a starting chest |
| Bronze | +2 | 1s 50b – 1s 75b | Wooden Anvil |
| Iron | +4 | 10s – 20s | Iron Anvil |
| Steel | +6 | 1g 20s – 2g 50s | Steel Anvil |

A zombie holding a higher-tier tool **works faster at its station** — that is the real reason to upgrade beyond your own belt.

## Which tools speed up which zombie station

This is the detail the tooltips bury: higher-tier tools make specific zombie stations run faster.

| Tool | Zombie station it speeds up |
|---|---|
| Iron Axe / Steel Axe | Lumberjack stand |
| Iron Pickaxe / Steel Pickaxe | Zombie Stone Quarry, Zombie Marble Quarry, Zombie Miner |
| Iron Shovel / Steel Shovel | Clay Digger Stand, Sand Digger Stand |

That is why one Iron Pickaxe is often the best tool purchase in the game: it accelerates three production stations at once, including the [iron](/items/iron-ore/) and [marble](/items/marble/) quarries.

## What to buy and when

- **Bronze everything first.** 1s 50b–1s 75b per tool is cheap, and +2 mastery is the biggest jump per coin.
- **Iron the tools you use hourly** — axe, pickaxe, shovel. Iron the rest only when the anvil is idle.
- **Steel is a quest and endgame flex**, not a mid-game upgrade; 1g 20s is real money in the same phase you need [order slots and factory parts](/guides/money-guide/).
- **Zombie tools are an investment**, not a purchase: a faster miner pays back in ore.
