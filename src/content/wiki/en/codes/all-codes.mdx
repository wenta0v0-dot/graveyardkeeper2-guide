## What actually speeds the game up

Graveyard Keeper 2 is an automation game wearing a graveyard costume. The intended "cheat code" is the tech tree, and three lines matter most:

### 1. Zombie workers — the real infinite-money glitch

Zombie workers take over the repetitive production work: mining, farming, factory stations. Every task you move off your own back becomes passive output. The mid-game turning point is not a bigger number — it is **how much of your day the dead are doing for you**.

### 2. Factory and alcohol lines

Processed goods out-earn raw goods. Once your factory and alcohol production lines are set, they convert cheap inputs into high-value products around the clock. Automate the input, automate the output, and collect.

### 3. Church income

Sermons and church work turn your graveyard quality into a steady faith and money engine. It is the income stream that runs on rails — worth building early and maintaining always.
