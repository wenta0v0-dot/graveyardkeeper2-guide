Fishing in Graveyard Keeper 2 is 22 species across **six spots** and **four baits**, and the difference between a wasted afternoon and a profitable one is knowing which bait opens which fish. Everything below is checked **October 27, 2026** (version 1.010).

## What to do with the catch

- **Sell the river and sea fish.** Roach through Trout all have buyers; that is the reliable coin line for the [early money guide](/guides/money-guide/).
- **The four legendary fish are not for sale.** Each turns into a signature dish instead: Kraken steak, Heavenly salmon, Elder's meal and Fried golden carp. They are the reason to fish with Live Bait at all.
- **Fillets feed cooking.** River fish give river fillets, sea fish give sea fillets, and the mysterious catches give their own cut — used in Fish nuggets, Baked fish, Fish soup, Baked salmon and Fisherman's pie.
- **Frog is the odd one out.** It bites on no bait, Breadcrumb and Insect, which makes it the easiest unusual catch in the Swamp.

## Practical fishing plan

1. **No bait first** — build kitchen stock from Roach and Gudgeon while your [fishing mastery](/guides/beginner-guide/) grows.
2. **Breadcrumb Bait** unlocks the money fish: Mackerel, Bream, Catfish, Silverfin.
3. **Insect Bait** is where the value is — Eel at 1s 92b is the best repeatable catch in the game.
4. **Live Bait only when hunting legendaries** — the fight values (17–20) are brutal without the Fisherman perk.
5. **Fish at night** for Crucian, Mackerel, Carp, Salmon and Swamp Ghost; that is where the "best by night" lines pay off.

Community data records a **99.3% bite chance** at the Inquisition Base with no bait at night — the safest place to practise the timing minigame before spending bait.
