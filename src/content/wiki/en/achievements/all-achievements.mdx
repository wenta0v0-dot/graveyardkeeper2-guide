Every Graveyard Keeper 2 achievement in one page, with the sourcing stated up front: the 38 names come from the game's own data, and the unlock conditions are quoted from the **platform trophy text** (the PlayStation list, published October 9) rather than any site's paraphrase of it — because a site's paraphrase is exactly how this page's first version got several rows wrong. That first version has been corrected and the correction is logged at the bottom of this page, dated **October 17, 2026**, version 1.010.

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  15 story achievements you earn by playing the quest line, 5 story ones Steam hides (their conditions are known anyway), and 18 grind counters that the [zombie workforce](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/) mostly clears by itself. The only Steam/PlayStation difference is one trophy slot — everything else matches 1:1.
</Callout>

## The Steam/PlayStation difference

One platform note before the tables:

- **One slot differs.** The Steam build data carries **Open for Business** where the PlayStation list carries **Business Has Begun** — every other name matches 1:1.
- **Two renames.** PlayStation spells them *The Alcohol Baron* and *The Iron Age*; the game data (Steam side) uses *Alcohol Baron* and *Iron Age*.
- **Totals.** Steam/Xbox: 38 achievements, 1,000 Gamerscore. PlayStation: 38 + the **Professional Keeper** Platinum = 39 trophies, 1,305 points.
- Conditions below are the PlayStation wording; Steam's exact strings may differ slightly. Where that matters, the table says so.

| Two faces | Meet Januarius and Jully — and notice they are the same person. |

| A Night to Remember | Attend Linda's concert (*Concept for a Concert*). |

## The 18 grind achievements

| Achievement | Unlock condition |
|---|---|
| Tree Bane | Cut down 100 trees. |
| True Graveyard Keeper | Bury 50 corpses (graveyard burials only). |
| Bronze Age | Melt 100 bronze ingots (furnace: 3 Bronze Scrap + 10 Fuel → 2; Bronze Forge: 3 Copper Nugget + 1 Tin + 20 Fuel → 4). |
| Iron Age | Melt 100 iron ingots (behind Iron Forge). |
| Master Farmer | Harvest 100 garden beds (bed crops count; trellis crops don't). |
| Master Cook | Cook 100 dishes (multi-portion recipes count per dish — Baked Apples is 5, or 7 with the Cook perk). |
| Fish Magnet | Catch 50 fish (6 spots, 22 species). |
| Novice Alchemist | Brew 25 times at the laboratory. |
| Alcohol Baron | Produce 100 bottles of alcohol (a Brewing Stand craft is 5 bottles; 8 with the Brewer perk). |
| Supplier | **Create** 50 Supply items (crafting counts — delivery not required). |
| Warmaker | **Craft** 50 pieces of war equipment (your gear and zombie squads' gear). |
| Necromancer | Resurrect 50 zombies (a corpse with all six vital organs + Reanimation Liquid + Faith + a collar rated for its red skulls). |
| Shepherd of Souls | Preach 50 sermons (Wrath only; the congregation is the lower of Likes and Church Quality). |
| Stroke of Genius | **Claim** 150 Inspirations (71 inspiration lines, 382 levels — progress must be claimed in the menu). |
| Home Sweet Tomb | Reach graveyard quality 200 (empty graves reduce it; Urn Hall II adds 20, the road upgrade 10). |
| Patron of merchants | Build all 11 Town shops (7 stall plots + 4 workshop courtyards; the first build only, no upgrades needed). |
| Urban Planner | Restore 15 Town houses (repairs also pay Green Scrolls). |
| Merchants' Best Friend | Complete 25 merchant orders (Pride only, two at a time, Urgent ones are weekly). |

## The five Steam hides — conditions confirmed

<Callout type="warn" title="Story spoilers below">
  These five are late-game story payoffs. Stop here if you want the ending fresh.
</Callout>

Steam ships no condition text for these five. The PlayStation list states all of them openly, so this page quotes those instead of the community rumors it carried before:

| Achievement | Condition (per the PlayStation trophy text) |
|---|---|
| The Ancient One | Find the Old God (*Friend or Foe*). The Red Eye choice matters: give it away and the finale needs **90 reputation** with all six friends; keep it and it needs **100**, plus extra requests. |
| An Unlikely Pair | Agatha and Herbert find common ground (*A Good Example is the Best Sermon*, after *Organic Music* and *The House at the Crossroads*). |
| Two Broken Souls | Jack and Gunter, mended (*I Love You, Man*: the Prosthetic Hand and the Small Printing Press). |
| A Dangerous Romance | Linda and Albert (*Love Story*, after *Heal the Healer*). |
| The Other Keeper | Meet the other Keeper, after *The Last Battle*. |
