Philosopher Stone is the endgame alchemy flex: a 3/3/3 rune spread, a Laboratory II requirement, and a conversion that turns ordinary ore into gold. If your [factory](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) is producing iron faster than you can spend it, this is where the surplus goes. Checked **October 24, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Runes needed | **3 red, 3 green, 3 blue** (exact match) |
| Laboratory | **Laboratory II** — three ingredients required |
| Working mixes | 398 documented combinations |
| Unlocked by | **Alchemy, Advanced** technology |
| Sells for | ~35 silver 75 bronze (Alchemist from level 3) |
| Used at | the **Formulation Bench** |

## What it converts

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 1 **Iron Ore** | 1 **Golden nugget** |
| 1 **Iron Ingot** | 1 **Golden ingot** |

Each conversion consumes **one Philosopher Stone**, so the real cost of a golden ingot is one stone plus one ingot. That trade is only worth it when iron is plentiful — which, once the [iron supply chain](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) is running, it usually is.

## Cheapest mixes

| Mix | Rune math |
|---|---|
| **Cheese + Cheese + Cheese** | 1R1G1B × 3 = 3R 3G 3B |
| Cheese + Tin + Honey | 1R1G1B + 2R 0G 1B + 0R 2G 1B |
| Cheese + Beeswax + Firefly | 1R1G1B + 1R 2G 0B + 1R 0G 2B |

All three cheapest routes lean on **Cheese**, which is the catch: cheese comes from quest rewards (Town Cheese Drop, Forest Pardon Cheese), not from a farm. Stockpile it early and treat it as the budget cap on this formula. The moment cheese runs out, the next-cheapest mixes jump in price quickly.

Backup inputs worth keeping: **Tin** (Sluice Box: 3 Sand), **Honey** and **Beeswax** (beehives, 3–6 honey and 1–2 beeswax), **Firefly** (rhododendron bushes, 40% chance), and **Melted Fat** as a red-heavy filler.

## How to brew it

1. **Build Laboratory II.** No one- or two-ingredient mix exists for a 3/3/3 target.
2. **Study your inputs.** The lab rejects un-studied ingredients.
3. **Balance the colours exactly.** Nine runes total, three of each — nine red or nine blue produces nothing.
4. **Brew blind if you like.** The first success records the formula in your Folio; the technology shortcut is Alchemy, Advanced.

## When it is worth it

- **Golden ingots** feed high-end crafting and the largest trade values — the return per stone is better than selling the stone itself.
- **Golden nuggets** are the cheaper conversion, useful when you have ore but not yet ingots.
- **Selling the stone** is the fallback: the Alchemist pays around 35 silver 75 bronze, which is a fair exit if you have no iron surplus.

The [money guide](/guides/money-guide/) puts this in the wider order: Paint for early coin, town orders for scale, Philosopher Stone for converting late-game surplus.
