Graveyard Keeper 2 is being patched fast — nine updates in the first eight weeks after the September 22 launch — and the patch notes matter more than usual, because several of them changed the economy and killed progression-blocking bugs. This page condenses every update into a dated log, sourced from the official announcement feed. Last checked **November 14, 2026**; this page is re-verified after every patch, the same promise the [codes page](/codes/all-codes/) makes.

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  Version trail: launch hotfix (Sep 22) → **1.005** (Sep 24) → **1.006** (Sep 25) → **1.007** (Sep 28) → **1.008** (Oct 2) → **1.009** (Oct 6) → **1.010** (Oct 13) → **1.011** (Oct 30) → **1.012** (Nov 13). If you stalled on a progression bug before 1.008, try again — the main-quest softlock and the worst crashers were fixed. The delivery economy got buffed in 1.008, order slots grew to 5 in 1.010, 1.011 brought moveable work stations and zombie autocrafting, and **1.012 reworks the PR Machine into a set-and-forget automation**.
</Callout>

## What each update actually changed

### Update 1.005 — the morgue and stations

- Fixed the **Study Table locking up** after inserting Science — the freeze that looked like a broken save.
- Fixed prepared bodies and strapped corpses resetting state, corpses stuck in the Crematorium, and zombies **failing to resurrect**.
- Rebalanced sawing output (sawing gives more logs) and the Kiln/Furnace split.
- Added the missing zombie-limit hint on PlayStation.

### Update 1.006 — bug reporting and the economy

- **In-game bug reporting** on Steam PC.
- Winning fights now earns **red tech points** (previously wasted effort).
- Fixed **fixed-price trade goods duplicating** on the first purchase of a session.
- **Demo-save milestones now unlock Steam achievements retroactively** — the biggest quality-of-life news for anyone who played the September demo.
- Defender's Emblems can no longer be destroyed (this protects your zombie squad unlocks), plus ultrawide and crop-bed fixes.

### Changelog 1.007 — crashes and duplication

- A wave of crash fixes: repeated exception popups, picking up the zombie caretaker, characters wedged in scenery.
- **Zombie fights now earn red tech points** for the player.
- Duplication and exploit fixes: bag-to-bag copying, alchemy duplication, and the dynamite destroy-and-sell exploit.
- Fixed the **~2 FPS freeze while saving**.
- Fixed a quest translation blocking a quest, the PR machine, iron-supply delivery quests, and self-study books.

### Update 1.008 — the delivery economy rebalance

This is the patch that changed how money works:

- **Fewer crates needed per delivery** and **more likes from deliveries** — the [factory supply chain](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) got meaningfully more profitable.
- **More likes from vendor trading** and a larger **Happy Supplier** perk bonus.
- **More Town Gratitude from winning battles.**
- Fixed: the going-to-bed crash, the **infinite loading screen** (conveyor exception), Town Warehouse and vine-cellar collisions, fast travel during battle, and a **main-quest softlock** — see the [walkthrough's save-before-the-choice warning](/quests/main-quest-walkthrough/) for the spot that still deserves a hard save.

### Changelog 1.009 — crashes, HUD locks and zombie stations

- Fixed a **crash while loading a save** and the player or HUD staying **locked after returning to the main menu** or starting a new game.
- Fixed the **day not rolling over** when the clock landed exactly on the end of the day — it showed up as a day jump in the quests UI after a sermon.
- Fixed the **nun's base upgrade softlock**, plus collisions around conveyor storage, the crematorium and the resurrection table.
- Zombie stations: sawmill, mine, clay pit and sand pit workers no longer get stuck after carrying results back; caretakers can be picked up or inspected mid-shift; a gardener whose harvest keeps failing returns to its station and leaves the crop for you.
- Existing-save corrections: the pre-order **Sauna debuff** no longer permanently reduces energy food restores, and the tier-2 oven stays hidden from the tech tree until it is repaired.
- This version also rolled out to **PlayStation and Xbox**.

### Update 1.010 — the softlock sweep and bigger order books

- **Order slots now rise up to 5** depending on villager count — a direct buff to the [town order income](/guides/money-guide/).
- **Belt Master perk** zombies give more gears, and the zombie porter no longer walks through factory walls.
- A roughly twenty-fix softlock sweep: stuck behind the bed after loading, the dungeon from the Town Warehouse, the sewer fight, crypt building, falling through the Floating Village, and more.
- Fixed the consistent **crash in Battle Operation Potato Part I** — the route the Act 6 Envy fragment runs through.
- Fixed inspirations requiring more actions than the world allows, the NPC "..." chatter bug, and several controller issues.

### Update 1.011 — the quality-of-life update (the big one)

- **Moveable work stations** — placed objects can now be moved in build mode. The most-requested feature since launch; no more destroying and rebuilding to fix a Workyard layout.
- **Zombie assistants can autocraft** — they now start and finish crafts at the Yard furnaces and kiln, the kitchen oven, the garden compost pile, cider and wine production in the vineyard basement, and the alchemy Distillation Cube. They earn tech points per craft but still need the mastery to start it. The [zombie economy](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/) just got a second gear.
- **In-game mod menu** — enable/disable mods in a click, and BepInEx now ships with the game. (Check your mods for compatibility before enabling after this update.)
- **Quarry zombie workers haul stone and marble to chests themselves** — the quarry zombie-assistant is no longer needed.
- **Zombies can now be cremated** — the first roadmap item to ship, alongside order slots in 1.010.
- Economy: **vendors restock faster**, advanced flasks are cost-effective, grape juice crafts easier, and Fine Jewelry (Jeweler III) no longer sells for 1 bronze.
- Quality-of-life: ultrawide support, queue total-output display, bumpers move items 10 at a time, conveyor belts place facing up.
- Notable fixes: the **Resurrection Room gate locking after 1.009.1** (the Envy-day resurrection room), **Albert's zombie breaking the zombie limit and its debuff**, Key Collector IV stuck at 23/24, slow ladder climbing, and a long-playtime crash.

### Update 1.012 — the PR Machine rework and rest options

- **PR Machine fully automated** — load Hypno-devices and Newspapers in any quantity and the machine runs campaigns for as long as the queue lasts; no more back-and-forth reloading. It can also now be **upgraded on any day using Graveyard quality points** (items still load on their exact day).
- **Campfire bench rest** — sit on the bench by the campfire next to the Keeper's house to restore energy and skip time. It does **not** save progress or clear insanity / the 'Lack of Sleep' debuff.
- **Richer map** — the map now shows morgue body counts, current and maximum zombies, and available order numbers.
- **Mercenaries accept wine or coin** and can join the final battle even if unpaid.
- **Memory usage optimization** and the PR Manager inspiration now counts each day the machine ran.
- Zombie-line fixes that matter to the [automation economy](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/): zombies no longer stop when the first queued craft lacks resources, auto-crafting no longer sticks when the queue is modified, supply carriers collect again, and stove cooking resumes.
- Trader stock: **the Apothecary now sells beeswax** and the tavern sells more exhumation certificates per level.
- Battle and story fixes: final sewer crash, final-fight pathfinding (zombies through the stone fence), unclaimable rewards at *The House at the Crossroads*, monks not granting the Monk's sword, and the *Personal Zombie Zone* placement bug. Lost items can be recovered from the **Crown Nest** near your home.

## What the developers have planned

The official roadmap post (undated items) listed: **zombie helpers**, **additional order slots**, **trader upgrades**, **Panic Reduction**, **rechargeable Hypno-devices**, and **zombie cremation**. Score so far: order slots shipped in 1.010, zombie cremation and zombie autocrafting in 1.011, and 1.012 automated the PR Machine (Hypno-devices are consumed from a queue now — though not the "rechargeable" kind). Still outstanding: trader upgrades, Panic Reduction, rechargeable Hypno-devices, zombie helpers beyond assistants. No multiplayer or co-op announcement.
