About

Last updated: 2026-08-11

Break and Steal an Egg Wiki is a fan-made community wiki dedicated to Break and Steal an Egg, a Simulation on Roblox developed by ILGames x Eggs. Our mission is to provide accurate, up-to-date guides, tips, and reference material to help players get the most out of the game.

What We Cover

  • Boss strategies and attack patterns
  • Item locations, stats, and crafting recipes
  • Active redeemable codes (updated daily)
  • Tier lists and build recommendations
  • Progression guides for new and returning players

Community-Driven

This wiki is maintained by volunteers. If you spot an error or have content to contribute, join our Discord and let us know. All content is freely accessible — no account required.
