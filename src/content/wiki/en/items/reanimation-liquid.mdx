Reanimation Liquid sits at the center of Graveyard Keeper 2's economy: it is the item that turns a prepared corpse into a worker that never sleeps. That makes it the one brew where ingredient cost genuinely compounds — every unit you save is a unit toward the [50-zombie Necromancer counter](/achievements/all-achievements/). Runes, mixes and prices checked **October 24, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Runes needed | **2 red, 1 green, 4 blue** (exact match) |
| Laboratory | **Laboratory II** — three ingredients, no 1- or 2-ingredient mix exists |
| Working mixes | 556 documented combinations |
| Unlocked by | the **Reanimation Liquid** technology |
| Sells for | bought by **Gunter** and the **Alchemist** |
| Consumed by | one zombie revival per unit |

## Cheapest mixes

The documented mixes are ordered by combined ingredient price, cheapest first. The top three:

| Mix | Rune math |
|---|---|
| **Tin + Peat + Night flower** | 2R 1B + 1G 1B + 2B = 2R 1G 4B |
| Minced Meat + Tin + Peat | 2B + 2R 1B + 1G 1B |
| Copper Nugget + Night flower + Night flower | 2R 1G + 2B + 2B |

The first line is the mix to build your stock around. All three inputs are renewable:

- **Tin** — Sluice Box: 3 Sand (needs the Bronze Forge).
- **Peat** — Compost Pile: 8 Leaves (needs Fertilizers I). Leaves come two at a time from every garden bed harvest.
- **Night flower** — night flower plants (2–3 per node), or demolish a Flower Bed III.

That is a loop you can run without spending coin: leaves from farming feed peat, sand feeds tin, and the wild plants respawn. Compare it against buying finished liquid from Gunter early on — before Laboratory II exists, buying is the only option anyway.

## How to brew it

1. **Study each ingredient first.** The lab refuses un-studied ingredients; studying costs Science plus 1–5 faith for most of them.
2. **Build Laboratory II.** Laboratory I caps at two ingredients and can never produce this formula.
3. **Match the runes exactly.** 2 red, 1 green, 4 blue — one rune over or under produces nothing. The lab sums colours; it does not read ingredient names.
4. **Check the Folio.** If you brew it blind, the first success records the formula in your Folio permanently.

## What it is actually for

One unit per zombie revival. The full cost of a worker is more than the liquid:

- a corpse with all six vital organs,
- one Reanimation Liquid,
- **Faith**, and
- a **Zombie Collar** rated for the corpse's red skulls.

Because the liquid is the only part of that list you can mass-produce, it is the part worth optimizing. The [zombie workers guide](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/) covers the corpse side and the station setups; the [alchemy cheat sheet](/items/alchemy-runes-cheat-sheet/) has the rune values for every ingredient on this page.
