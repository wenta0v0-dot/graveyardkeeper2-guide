Marble is the resource that separates a decorated graveyard from a full one. Five deposits in the whole world, four pieces each — and the [quality-200 achievement](/achievements/all-achievements/) sits behind the kits you build from it. Checked **October 27, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Sells for | 50 bronze |
| Stack | 30 |
| Study | 5 tech points |
| Alchemy rune | **3 red** (8 formulas) |
| World supply | **5 deposits × 4** — only 1 on the map |
| Automation | Zombie Marble Quarry |

## Where to get Marble

**1. Marble deposits** — five nodes across the world, **4 marble each**, gated by **Building mastery 4 to 7**. Only one appears on the map, which is why players routinely report marble as "missing" — the other four have to be found on foot.

**2. Zombie Marble Quarry** — the automatic source: 8 ticks, Building mastery 4, +1 red tech point. It unlocks later than the stone quarry, so plan a manual sweep first.

There is no trader selling raw marble, which makes the deposits worth clearing properly rather than opportunistically.

## What Marble is for

**The decoration chain**

| Output | Station | Marble per craft |
|---|---|---|
| Marble Kit | Assembly bench II | 2 |
| Sculpture Kit II | Assembly bench III | 4 |
| Marble Plate | Stonemason's Workbench | 2 |

**Plus eight alchemy formulas** as a red-rune carrier — including Healing Potion III, Berserker Potion III and [Philosopher Stone](/items/philosopher-stone/). If your lab is short on red runes, spare marble is a valid source; check the [alchemy cheat sheet](/items/alchemy-runes-cheat-sheet/) before spending it.

## How marble fits graveyard quality

The burial loop converts resources into rating: marble kits and sculpture kits raise the pieces around graves, and rating is what [the beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) calls the first engine. Two practical notes:

- **Clear all five deposits early** — 20 units total is enough to finish a decoration pass without waiting on the zombie quarry.
- **Do not sell marble for 50 bronze.** One Marble Kit is worth more than the marble it eats once the rating translates into [sermon bonuses](/guides/prayers-and-sermons-guide/).
