Blue Crystal is the quiet cap on your zombie workforce: every collar needs one, and collars decide which corpses are even usable. If your [zombie economy](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/) has stalled at "not enough collars", this is why. Checked **October 27, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Buys for | **75 bronze** (Gunter) |
| Sells for | 54 bronze |
| Stack | 20 |
| Study | 5 tech points |
| Alchemy rune | **1 green, 2 blue** (6 formulas) |
| Free source | Swamp statue pile, once per **Envy** day |

## Where to get Blue Crystal

**1. Gunter (reliable)** — 75 bronze each. This is the price of progress; buy when a collar is the only thing standing between you and the next worker.

**2. The Goddess of Nature statue (free)** — a pile of **4** appears by the statue in the Swamp **at the start of every Envy**, on one condition: the previous pile was collected. That gives a steady four per Envy cycle essentially for free.

**The known gap**: after the *Goddess in the Swamp* ritual and before you ask the statue **"The Man with the Red Eye"**, the pile stops spawning. The reported fix is simply to ask that question — then the loop resumes.

Third-source note: there is no crafting recipe and no zombie station for it, so those two routes are the only supply.

## What Blue Crystal is for

**The collars — the real reason it matters**

| Collar | Station | Crystal per craft |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze Zombie Collar | Jewelry Table | 1 |
| Golden Zombie Collar | Jewelry Table | 1 |
| Steel Zombie Collar | Jewelry Table | 1 |

Each collar carries a red-skull rating, and a worker needs a collar that covers its corpse's skulls. That is why crystal supply gates the whole [Necromancer counter](/achievements/all-achievements/) — 50 revivals means 50 collars means 50 crystals.

**Alchemy** — six formulas use it as the green-blue carrier: Reanimation Liquid, Alkali, Umami, Spices, Berserker Potion III and [Philosopher Stone](/items/philosopher-stone/). Values are in the [cheat sheet](/items/alchemy-runes-cheat-sheet/).

## The routine that keeps collars flowing

1. **Finish Heart of the Swamp** to start the statue loop.
2. **Ask the statue "The Man with the Red Eye"** early to avoid the spawn gap.
3. **Collect every Envy** — four free crystals per cycle.
4. **Top up from Gunter** when you are about to revive more than one worker.
