Graveyard Keeper 2 Alchemy Cheat Sheet — Runes, Formulas, Ingredients

Every Graveyard Keeper 2 alchemy formula with its exact red/green/blue rune total, the ingredient rune table, and the lab rules the game never explains — dated.

10/17/2026 1.009 GK2 Guide Last updated: 10/17/2026 8 min read

Quick Answer

Alchemy in Graveyard Keeper 2 is arithmetic, not recipes: every ingredient carries red, green and blue rune values, and a brew works only when the totals match a formula exactly. This cheat sheet lists all 29 formulas with their rune totals, the ingredient values verified so far, and the lab rules the game never explains.

FAQ

How does alchemy work in Graveyard Keeper 2?
Every ingredient carries red, green and blue rune values. The lab adds them up colour by colour, and a mix produces a result only when the totals match a formula exactly — one rune too many or too few makes nothing.
How do I see an ingredient's runes?
Study one of it at the Study Table — it costs Science and, for most ingredients, 1 to 5 faith. Alchemic Powders are the exception: their runes are known from the start and match their names.
How many ingredients can I mix at once?
Laboratory I takes up to 2 ingredients, Laboratory II up to 3. Six formulas have no 2-ingredient mix and require Laboratory II.
Are Clay and Sand interchangeable?
Yes — both carry exactly 1 red rune, and colour is the only thing the lab reads. Any two ingredients with identical rune values are fully interchangeable.

Graveyard Keeper 2 does not have alchemy recipes in the first game’s sense — it has arithmetic. Every ingredient carries red, green and blue runes; the lab sums them colour by colour; and a mix only produces something when the totals match a formula exactly. Once you read the tables below, you never need to brute-force a brew again. Checked against community-documented game data current to version 1.009 (October 17, 2026).

The short version

Match the rune totals exactly — 2 green makes Paint, 1 red + 1 blue makes Zombie Motivator, one rune off makes sludge. Study an ingredient to reveal its runes (Alchemic Powders are pre-known). Laboratory I mixes 2 ingredients, Laboratory II mixes 3 — and six formulas, including Reanimation Liquid, only work there.

How the rune system actually works

The rules the lab UI never states:

  • Exact match or nothing. Runes add up colour by colour. One rune too many or too few produces nothing — there is no partial success.
  • Laboratory I takes 2 ingredients, Laboratory II takes 3. 23 of 29 formulas have a 1- or 2-ingredient mix; 35 mixes are a single ingredient.
  • Studying reveals runes. Study one of an ingredient at the Study Table — it costs Science, plus 1–5 faith for 38 of the 43 studyable ingredients. Un-studied ingredients are refused by the lab. Faith planning matters here too.
  • Alchemic Powders are pre-known. All 19 of them carry exactly the runes in their names — no studying needed.
  • A mix never exceeds 5 runes of one colour — the reason Healing Potion III (5 red) is the ceiling for pure-red brews.
  • Colour is the only thing that matters. Clay and Sand are both 1 red, and the lab treats them as the same thing.
  • Cacao has runes but the lab never accepts it. A known quirk — do not stockpile it for brewing.
  • Brewing an unknown formula still works. The first successful brew records it in the Folio; 25 of 29 formulas are also unlockable by technology, but four (the tier-3 battle potions) are brew-only.

Battle potions, and what they actually do

The 12 combat formulas break down into four families of three tiers:

  • Healing I–III (2 / 4 / 5 red): restore 20 / 40 / 100 of the Keeper’s health.
  • Damage I–III (1r1g / 2r2g / 3r3g): +3 / +6 / +9 damage per hit for 30 seconds — applies to the Keeper’s swords and bows and to upgraded mercenaries’ gear, not to zombie fighters or archer towers.
  • Zombie Motivator I–III (1r1b / 1r2b / 3r3b): zombie fighters wait half as long between attacks, for 15s / 30s / 1 minute.
  • Berserker I–III (1r1g1b / 2r1g1b / 3r2g2b): sword and bow attacks cost no stamina for 15s / 30s / 45s.
