Graveyard Keeper 2 Main Quest Walkthrough — All 7 Acts in Order

The Graveyard Keeper 2 main quest line in order — the prison escape, Larry, all seven acts, every reputation gate and weekday lock that can stall your progress.

10/17/2026 1.009 GK2 Guide Last updated: 10/17/2026 8 min read

Quick Answer

Graveyard Keeper 2 runs 41 journal quests across seven acts, book-ended by an unjournaled prologue: the prison escape, Larry, the sewers and your first burial. This walkthrough lists every act in order with its quest names, the reputation and quality gates that stall progress, and the weekday locks the game explains nowhere.

FAQ

How many main quests are in Graveyard Keeper 2?
41 journal quests across seven acts, plus an unjournaled prologue (prison escape through first burial). Some counts say 40 because one journal step is a sub-step of another quest.
What reputation do I need for the ending?
90+ reputation with six friends for the finale — 100 on some Act 6 routes. Jack is the steepest climb (60 for the foundry, 80 for the Pride fragment, 90+ at the end).
Why can't I preach or accept town orders today?
Weekday locks. Sermons only work on Wrath days, accepting town orders on Pride days, resurrections and Blue Crystals on Envy days, and most town battles on Sloth days. The first sermon of the game is the only sermon that works on any day.
Do my choices change the ending?
The Red Eye choice in Act 6 and the final Go-or-Stay choice both branch the story — and keeping the eye has produced blocked endings for some players on 1.008, so save before it.

Spoiler notice

This page walks the entire main story in order — quest names, gates and story beats included. If you want the discoveries fresh, stop after the act you are stuck on.

Graveyard Keeper 2’s story is 41 journal quests across seven acts, book-ended by a prologue the journal does not track. The real gates are not quest items — they are reputation thresholds, quality targets and weekday locks. This walkthrough lists every act in order with the numbers you need to clear it. Structure checked against community-documented quest data current to version 1.009 (October 17, 2026).

Prologue: the prison, Larry and the sewers

The game opens with your character imprisoned, and the opening hour teaches every system through escape:

  1. The prison escape — you are looking for a way out; the route runs through the sewers.
  2. Larry — the talking skull you meet on the way out, and the voice that follows you for the rest of the game.
  3. Crown Hill — the sewer exit delivers you to the graveyard that becomes yours.
  4. The town defence — the first battle. The common mistake: the game requires you to equip the Rusty Armor and Rusty Sword, not just carry them.
  5. The first burial — the full routine: dig, fill, and place the Wooden Sign. It ends with the Burial Certificate I, which you will need almost immediately.

Act 1: Settling In (8 quests)

Gates: Graveyard quality 10 · 3 Faith · Barracks quality 12

  • Certificate, Please — Herbert checks you are carrying Burial Certificate I (sell it afterwards).
  • Dust in the Kitchen — a self-completing Fairy quest.
  • Grave Expectations — reach Graveyard quality 10.
  • Fighting Spirit Required — deliver 3 Faith, which means your first sermon (via Holy Moly!).
  • Dungeons and… that’s it, probably. — clear the dungeon blockage (3 Bronze Detail, 3 Wooden Plank); needs 10 Village reputation via Good Fellow.
  • Sweet Dreams — brew 1 Dream Dust, then create your first two zombies.
  • Swords and Bell — Barracks quality 12 plus the Magic bell (1 Blood + 1 Bronze Ingot, Smithing 3).
  • First… Sludge? — win Break Through to the Port (12 power). The factory line sits behind this.

Unlocks: the Village, the graveyard expansion, the dungeon and Alchemy Lab, your first zombies, the first Town district.

Act 2: The Port and the Fairy (3 quests)

Gates: 10 Gunter reputation · Farming mastery 3

  • Docked and Found — meet Jack; opens the bridge and the Town Warehouse.
  • Friendship Written in the Stars — get Gunter’s Deck of Cards (via Linda’s Safe Harbor); the 10 Gunter reputation this grants is what unlocks his three zombies.
  • Chasing Fairy Lights — summon the Fairy (1 Dream Dust, 1 Comet’s Tail, 1 Tooth; Farming mastery 3). The reward is the Fairy’s Swamp Map.

Act 3: Forest, Swamp and the Goddess (7 quests)

Gate: the Royal Pardon (5 silver + 3 Ink)

  • Into the Forest (Eventually) — the guards turn you away without a pass.
  • Into the Forest — repair the Royal Postbox, obtain the Temporary Head of the Village Certificate, and get the Forest Pass.
  • Collect Head of the Village certificate. — a journal sub-step of the quest above (this is why some counts say 40 quests, not 41).
  • Forest Pass — explore the Forest to the bandits Hans and Sven.
  • Go Wild in the Wild Lands — the Royal Pardon plus the Swamp Map open the Swamp bridge.
  • Heart of the Swamp — meet the Goddess of Nature; weekly Blue Crystals on Envy days.
  • Any Portal in a Storm — investigate the broken portal; this thread leads to the Bishop in Act 5.

Act 4: The Village Election (6 quests)

Gates: Herbert 60 · Linda 60 · Gunter 50 · 100 Faith for the ritual

  • Election Fever — Januarius hands out campaign jobs.
  • Security Checkpoint: Democracy — Herbert at 55, then 60.
  • Vote Early, Vote Naughty — Linda at 55, then 60.
  • Destiny Polls — Gunter at 45, then 50.
  • Campaign of Thrones — vote between the twins Heffry and Jeffry; talk to Jack and Agatha before you leave.
  • Goddess in the Swamp — bring Jully 100 Faith, the largest single faith cost in the story. Plan two strong sermons ahead (see the faith guide).

Hidden prerequisites the journal does not show: Safe zone needs three battles won; Plague Me Maybe needs Albert at 50; Stars, Lies, & Contracts needs Jack at 60.

Act 5: Royal Foundry and First Fragments (6 quests)

Gates: Jack 60 · battles at 56 / 80 / 88 power

  • Front-of-the-Envelope Calculation — requires Jack at 60.
  • Water Locked — win Closing the Western Floodgate (56 power, Sloth day).
  • Steamy Royal — Eastern Floodgate I and II (80 power), then Break Through to the Bathhouse Basement (88 power) → the Royal Foundry and the Bishop.
  • Magic Mirror — pay 3 Zombie Power (15 Wheat) for the Sloth portal fragment.
  • The Hungry Cosmos — Gunter gives the Gluttony fragment.
  • High Price — buy the Lust fragment from the Trademaster for 1 gold — Lust day only.

Each fragment must be installed at the Astrologer’s Tower; installation triggers the next quest.

Act 6: The Last Fragments (6 quests)

Gates: Jack 80 · the story’s major choice

  • Finding Old God — Davy’s trail ends in the Sewers.
  • Friend or Foe — after resting, Dig delivers the Red Eye. The choice: give it back (needs a sermon to 180 parishioners on a Wrath day) or keep it (needs Fix the Attic and the Dark talents).
  • Eye, Red Eye — the consequence/preparation route for your choice.
  • Green Fragment — the Envy fragment from the drained Sluice Area (Operation POTATO battles).
  • More Portal Parts — the Pride fragment from Jack at reputation 80 (Man of Steel + Factory Tour bridge the 60→80 climb).
  • Even More Portal Parts — the Wrath fragment at your Inquisition Base; opens Palace Politics.

Save before the Red Eye choice

Keeping the eye after delivering all six first-stage gifts has produced blocked endings for some players on 1.008. Make a hard save before Friend or Foe.

Act 7: The Palace and the Ending (5 quests)

Gates: 90+ reputation with six friends (100 on some routes) · 144 defense power

  • Palace Politics — pay Davy 50 silver for intelligence.
  • Gluttony and Pride — Jack and Gunter.
  • Lust and Envy — Linda and Albert.
  • Wrath and Sloth — Agatha and Herbert.
  • The Last Battle — 144 defense, up to 4 squads, Sloth day only. Afterwards: take the Greed fragment from the other Keeper in the Palace, install it, and choose Go or Stay.

The reputation ladder

The finale needs 90+ with six friends (100 on some routes). The story’s asks, per friend:

Friend Story checkpoints Finale
Jack 60 (foundry) · 70 (Steel Smithing) · 80 (Pride fragment) 90–100
Gunter 10 (zombies) · 45→50 (election) 90–100
Herbert 55→60 (election) 90–100
Agatha 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 across her quests 90–100
Linda 55→60 (election) 90–100
Albert 20 (violin) · 50 (Linda’s visit) 90–100

Weekday locks — the calendar the game explains nowhere

The six-day week locks activities to specific days. Plan the week around these:

Day Locked to that day
Sloth Most town battles, and the Final Battle
Lust Buying the Lust fragment · Linda’s concert
Envy The resurrection room · Blue Crystals (the Prayer for Storms lifts the lock for 90 minutes)
Pride Accepting town orders
Gluttony Feeding the PR Machine
Wrath Sermons — only the very first sermon of the game works on any day
