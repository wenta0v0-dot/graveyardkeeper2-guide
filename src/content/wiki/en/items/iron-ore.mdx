Iron Ore is the bottleneck that decides how fast your tools and [factory](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) scale — and unlike most resources, it has three distinct sources with very different costs. Numbers checked **October 27, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Buys for | **1 silver** (Trademaster) |
| Sells for | 72 bronze |
| Stack | 50 |
| Study | 6 tech points |
| Alchemy rune | **3 red** (8 formulas) |
| Best source | Zombie Miner, once coal is automated |

## Where to get Iron Ore

**1. Iron ore deposits (manual)** — 28 nodes are placed in the world, 3 ore each, and mining needs **Smithing mastery 3**. The **Miner** perk adds +1 per node. This is the early-game source; it is also where you feel the walk distance most.

**2. Zombie Miner (automatic)** — the production recipe is **Iron Ore + 2 Coal**, 8 ticks, Building mastery 1, +1 red tech point per cycle. Note what that means: your iron supply is really a **coal** supply. Automate coal first or the miner idles.

**3. Trademaster (buy it)** — 1 silver per ore. Buying ore to smelt is rarely worth it except to finish a tool tier tonight; buying 2 Coal is the cheaper fix for a stalled miner.

## What Iron Ore is for

**Smelting**

| Output | Station | Ore per craft |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Ingot | Smithy I | 1 |
| Iron Ingot | Furnace II | 3 |

The Furnace II route burns three ore per ingot but skips the smithy queue — worth it only when you have a true surplus.

**Everything else (11 documented uses)**

- **Golden nugget** — Formulation Bench, 1 Iron Ore + 1 [Philosopher Stone](/items/philosopher-stone/).
- **Eight alchemy formulas** as the red-rune carrier: Healing Potion II and III, Damage Potion III, Zombie Motivator Potion III, Berserker Potion III, [Organ Improver](/items/organ-improver/), Tannin Liquid and Philosopher Stone.

Because the alchemy uses do not consume iron, its rune value matters more than its quantity there — see the [alchemy cheat sheet](/items/alchemy-runes-cheat-sheet/).

## The build order that works

1. **Manual mining** until Smithing 3 and your first Bronze tools.
2. **Automate coal** (Zombie Miner consumes it) — the [zombie workers guide](/guides/zombie-workers-guide/) has the station setup.
3. **Add a second Zombie Miner** once the [factory](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) iron chain is running.

Selling ore is a trap: 72 bronze looks like easy money until you count what one ingot becomes. Sell finished goods, not ore.
