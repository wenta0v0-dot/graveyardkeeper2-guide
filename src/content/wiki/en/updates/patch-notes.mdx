Graveyard Keeper 2 is being patched fast — five updates in the first two weeks after the September 22 launch — and the patch notes matter more than usual, because several of them changed the economy and killed progression-blocking bugs. This page condenses every update into a dated log, sourced from the official announcement feed. Last checked **October 17, 2026**; this page is re-verified after every patch, the same promise the [codes page](/codes/all-codes/) makes.

## What each update actually changed

### Update 1.005 — the morgue and stations

- Fixed the **Study Table locking up** after inserting Science — the freeze that looked like a broken save.
- Fixed prepared bodies and strapped corpses resetting state, corpses stuck in the Crematorium, and zombies **failing to resurrect**.
- Rebalanced sawing output (sawing gives more logs) and the Kiln/Furnace split.
- Added the missing zombie-limit hint on PlayStation.

### Update 1.006 — bug reporting and the economy

- **In-game bug reporting** on Steam PC.
- Winning fights now earns **red tech points** (previously wasted effort).
- Fixed **fixed-price trade goods duplicating** on the first purchase of a session.
- **Demo-save milestones now unlock Steam achievements retroactively** — the biggest quality-of-life news for anyone who played the September demo.
- Defender's Emblems can no longer be destroyed (this protects your zombie squad unlocks), plus ultrawide and crop-bed fixes.

### Changelog 1.007 — crashes and duplication

- A wave of crash fixes: repeated exception popups, picking up the zombie caretaker, characters wedged in scenery.
- **Zombie fights now earn red tech points** for the player.
- Duplication and exploit fixes: bag-to-bag copying, alchemy duplication, and the dynamite destroy-and-sell exploit.
- Fixed the **~2 FPS freeze while saving**.
- Fixed a quest translation blocking a quest, the PR machine, iron-supply delivery quests, and self-study books.

### Update 1.008 — the delivery economy rebalance

This is the patch that changed how money works:

- **Fewer crates needed per delivery** and **more likes from deliveries** — the [factory supply chain](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) got meaningfully more profitable.
- **More likes from vendor trading** and a larger **Happy Supplier** perk bonus.
- **More Town Gratitude from winning battles.**
- Fixed: the going-to-bed crash, the **infinite loading screen** (conveyor exception), Town Warehouse and vine-cellar collisions, fast travel during battle, and a **main-quest softlock** — see the [walkthrough's save-before-the-choice warning](/quests/main-quest-walkthrough/) for the spot that still deserves a hard save.

### Update 1.009 — consoles

Confirmed rolling out on **PlayStation and Xbox** (October 2026). The PC changelog for this version is being tracked; this table updates as soon as it publishes.

## What the developers have planned

The official roadmap post (undated items) lists: **zombie helpers**, **additional order slots**, **trader upgrades**, **Panic Reduction**, **rechargeable Hypno-devices**, and **zombie cremation**. No dates, no multiplayer or co-op announcement.
