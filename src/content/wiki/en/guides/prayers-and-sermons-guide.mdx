Sermons are the engine behind [every Faith purchase in the game](/guides/faith-guide/), and the differences between them are not cosmetic: the gap between the best and worst sermon is roughly **six times the Faith per parishioner**. This page carries all 26 prayers and every one of the 58 sermon tiers, with the numbers that decide which one you preach this Wrath day. Checked **October 27, 2026** (version 1.010).

## How a sermon actually scores

Four rules do all the work:

1. **Attendance** = whichever is lower, your town's happiness or your church's quality. Graveyard quality is a separate stat — it scales the *success bonus money*, not how many people show up.
2. **Parishioners spent** = the sermon's **Start** value. Preaching spends that many parishioners, which also spends that much happiness in one go.
3. **Success chance** = parishioners × 100 ÷ the sermon's **Guaranteed** value, capped at 100%. A Guaranteed of 15 with 15 parishioners is an automatic win.
4. **Two payouts.** The guaranteed part pays Faith per parishioner and money per parishioner. On a success you get a second helping — extra Faith per parishioner plus money per point of graveyard quality — and the tier's buff or item. Miss the roll and you keep the base payout but lose the bonus and the buff.

**Three perks change the numbers**: **Priest** raises Faith from every sermon, **Cardinal** raises money, **Performer** boosts the success-only bonuses (both sides). All three come from the Theology tree and do nothing until you take them.

## The best picks, by what you need

| Goal | Winner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Most Faith per parishioner | **Prayer for Old God** — 1.5 (with Priest) | Needs 180 parishioners — the highest threshold in the game; a one-time quest gift, not research |
| Best repeatable Faith | **Faith Prayer III / Prayer for Repose III** — 0.8 (with Priest) | The tech-unlocked tiered prayers you build toward |
| Most money per parishioner | **4-way tie at 60** (with Cardinal): Old God, Obedience II, Obedience III, Music | Prayer for Music needs the fewest parishioners of the four, at 80 |
| Best success bonus, money | **Prayer for Likes III** — 72 (with Performer) | Drops hard under the Excessive Zombies debuff |
| Best success bonus, Faith | **Prayer for Demand III** — 0.8 (with Performer) | Highest success-only Faith rate in the list |
| Cheapest unlock | **Religious Writing I** — Faith Prayer + Money Prayer | 10 red / 10 green / 20 blue: the smallest research bill of the six unlock techs |
| Free unlocks | **Basic Prayer** and **Prayer for Old God** | Both quest gifts — no research at all |

- **Storms** — God of the storms, 90 minutes (single tier; lifts the Envy-day resurrection lock, per the [patch and mechanics notes](/quests/main-quest-walkthrough/)).
- **Obedience** — God of the zombies, 30 / 60 / 99:59.
- **Consumption** — God of offer, 30 / 60 / 99:59.
- **Demand** — God of demand, 30 / 60 / 99:59.
- **Likes** — Town Gratitude ×10 / ×20 / ×30.
- **Hypno** — God of delusions, 45 / 90 / 99:59 — this is the buff that cancels the Excessive Zombies penalty.
- **Prosperity** — Prosperity Item ×1 / ×3 / ×5.

## A practical sermon plan

- **First weeks** — Basic Prayer I. Start 4, Guaranteed 8: the cheapest way to learn the parishioner math before committing anything bigger.
- **Mid game, general** — Money Prayer II (Start 20, Guaranteed 40) once church quality supports 40 attendees.
- **When you need Faith** — Faith Prayer II, then Faith Prayer III (0.5 → 0.8 with Priest).
- **When you need coin** — Obedience II/III or Prayer for Music at Start 80, the cheapest route to the 60-per-parishioner money ceiling.
- **Never forget the debuff** — if you over-built zombies, every sermon loses a flat chunk until the count drops or you preach Hypno Prayer for God of delusions.

Sermons count toward two [achievements](/achievements/all-achievements/): **Words of Faith** (first sermon) and **Shepherd of Souls** (50 sermons), so the routine is not optional long-term.
