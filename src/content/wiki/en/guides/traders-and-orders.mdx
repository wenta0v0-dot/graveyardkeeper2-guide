The town's economy runs on two rails: **traders** (each with a weekly shopping list that pays Likes) and **supply orders** (Jack's board, which pays Likes and coin by the box). This page is the complete reference for both — every trader, every order, every cap — transcribed from game data current to **version 1.010** (October 30, 2026).

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  19 traders total: 4 you meet early, 3 special suppliers, and **12 town shops** that open one by one as you restore them. Orders are taken on **Pride days**, pay **Likes + coin per box of ten**, and **Urgent versions pay roughly double the Likes**. Update 1.010 raised order slots to **5** with villager count — the single biggest delivery-economy buff yet ([patch notes](/updates/patch-notes/)).
</Callout>

## How traders and Likes actually work

- Each trader keeps a **weekly shopping list** — specific items they want, in three tiers ("5 Likes for 5 sold", "10 for 10", "20 for 20"). Sell them what they ask for and you earn **Likes** up to their weekly cap.
- **Like caps** are per trader: **15 / 30 / 50 Likes per week** depending on the trader's own tier. Hitting the cap just means that trader is done paying Likes this week — they still buy your goods for coin.
- **Likes raise the trader's tier.** Tier unlocks higher-tier supply orders and richer stock, which is why early Likes are worth more than early coin.
- **Town traders refuse their own category** — the Baker won't buy bread, the Smithy won't buy hammers, the Jeweler won't buy talismans. Plan your routes around it.
- The village and port traders (Januarius, Herm, Trademaster, Gunter) and the specials (Jack, Bishop, Harold) work differently — details in their table rows below.

## All 76 supply orders

Orders come in **Regular** and **Urgent** flavors — Urgent pays roughly **double the Likes** for the same box. Every repeatable order is a box of **ten**; Jack's three starter boxes are the only small ones. All require the trader to be at the listed tier.

Pay table: **Likes (with Happy Supplier perk)** — coin is per box. The **Happy Supplier** perk (Theology/Supplier tree) adds roughly +25–50% Likes on every delivery, and [Update 1.008](/updates/patch-notes/) made it stronger.

Pay format: **Regular Likes / Urgent Likes · coin per box**. The Happy Supplier perk applies on top of both.

## What each Supply box takes to make

The boxes are crafted, not assembled from loose stock — examples from the crafting data:

- **Supply: Iron** — Woodworking Workbench I: 6 Iron Ingot + 4 Board + 8 Bronze Nails (8 ticks); or Assembly bench I: 8 Iron Ingot. Feeds the [iron](/items/iron-ore/) chain straight into orders.
- **Supply: Fabric** — Woodworking Workbench I: 4 Fabric; or Assembly bench I: 16 Flax.
- **Supply: Cutlery I** — Assembly bench II: 4 Iron Kit I.

Pattern: every box is a crafted tier product, which is why the [factory](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) and the order board are the same economy — the machines fill the boxes, Pride days sell them.

## Strategy notes

- **Urgent first, always** — same box, roughly double Likes. Urgent orders rotate per week; plan your Pride day around them.
- **Tier III boxes are the endgame pay** — 40–75 Likes and up to 2 silver per box, and with slots at 5 after 1.010 a full Pride day can clear five of them.
- **Likes before coin** early: a shop at tier III unlocks its 40/75 orders permanently, which beats any one-off sale.
- **Watch the refusals** — a full inventory of hammers is unsellable at the Smithy; split routes between shops.
- Achievement hooks: **Merchants' Best Friend** = 25 completed orders; **Supplier** counts **crafted** Supply items (50), not deliveries; **Patron of merchants** = all 11 town shops built.
