Every Graveyard Keeper 2 run hits the same walls, in roughly the same order. This page lists the blockers players ask about most, where each answer lives in-game, and what typically unsticks it — **checked against version 1.008 (October 16, 2026)**. Entries marked *in-game verification in progress* are being confirmed hands-on before we publish full walkthroughs; we would rather say that than guess.

<Callout type="info" title="The pattern behind most blockers">
  Almost every mid-game stall in Graveyard Keeper 2 traces back to one thing: **a tech-tree chain that should have been started days earlier**. When stuck, open the technology tree, find the unlock you need, and walk its prerequisites backwards. The foundry line is the longest.
</Callout>

## Steel and the Royal Foundry

The most-stuck point in the game. Steel sits behind the **Royal Foundry** tech line, and the line's prerequisite chain is long enough that players who ignore it for the early game hit a hard wall mid-run.

- **Where the answer lives**: the technology tree — find the Royal Foundry node and walk its prerequisites backwards.
- **What typically unsticks it**: starting the chain as early as the tree allows and letting each prerequisite mature while you run the graveyard and church engines.
- Deeper walkthrough: *in-game verification in progress* — this entry gets a full page once every step is confirmed on 1.008.

## Polished Bronze and Bronze Detail

A crafting-chain blocker: both products sit on the bronze line, and the stall is usually either a missing tech node or an input shortage further up the chain.

- **Where the answer lives**: the tech tree (bronze-line nodes) plus your input production.
- **What typically unsticks it**: securing the raw supply before the crafting node, not after.
- Deeper walkthrough: *in-game verification in progress*.

## Orders, Jack, and the warehouse

The orders system routes through **Jack and the warehouse**, and new keepers regularly misread what an order needs versus what the warehouse holds.

- **Where the answer lives**: the orders interface with Jack — it states the requirement directly.
- **What typically unsticks it**: treating the warehouse as the checkpoint between production and orders: production feeds the warehouse, orders draw from it.
- Deeper walkthrough: *in-game verification in progress*.

## How to get faith

Faith flows from the church engine: **graveyard quality → church work → sermons → faith**. If faith has stalled, the cause is almost always upstream in the graveyard, not in the church.

- **Where the answer lives**: the church and sermon systems, documented in our [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).
- **What typically unsticks it**: improving graveyard quality rather than grinding sermons harder.

## Zombie mining and automation

Zombie workers are the automation endgame: mining, farming and factory stations run by the dead. The unlock is worth beelining because it converts your active day into passive output.

- **Where the answer lives**: the tech tree (automation line) and the station interfaces themselves.
- **What typically unsticks it**: starting the automation line as soon as the tech tree shows it — it competes with the foundry line for your attention, and both are long.
- Deeper walkthrough: *in-game verification in progress*.

## Demo save transfer

Progress from the pre-release demo carries into the full game — the transfer is handled by the game itself on first launch of the full version.

- **What typically unsticks it**: launching the full game on the same platform account the demo was played on.
