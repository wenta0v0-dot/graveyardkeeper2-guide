Paint is the formula that makes alchemy feel like free money: its two inputs are the items you already have too many of, and the combination itself costs **no energy**. That is why it anchors the early income section of the [money guide](/guides/money-guide/). Runes, mixes and buyers checked **October 24, 2026** (version 1.010).

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Runes needed | **2 green** (exact match) |
| Laboratory | Laboratory I or II |
| Working mixes | 21 documented combinations |
| Unlocked by | the **Paint** technology |
| Sells for | ~1 bronze to 2 silver — Trademaster, Alchemist, Furniture Maker (level 3) |
| Energy cost at the lab | **none** |

## Cheapest mixes

Because the target is two green runes, **any two green carriers work**. The documented cheap mixes:

| Mix | Rune math |
|---|---|
| **Berry + Berry** | 1G + 1G |
| Leaf + Berry | 1G + 1G |
| Water + Water | 1G + 1G |
| Leaf + Leaf | 1G + 1G |
| Water + Leaf | 1G + 1G |
| Water + Berry | 1G + 1G |

There are also six single-ingredient options for when one material already carries two greens, which means you can brew Paint from a single stack without hunting for a partner.

Sourcing the inputs is the easy part:

- **Leaf** — two per garden bed harvest, so farming generates the stock passively.
- **Berry** — ashberry bushes, 2–4 per bush.
- **Water** — the well gives 10 per draw, 20 with Garden Well II.
- **Swamp mushroom** — green nodes, 2–3 per node, if you want a single-ingredient route.

## Why it earns

Three properties line up:

1. **Zero-energy combination.** Brewing Paint does not spend the resource that limits everything else in your day.
2. **Inputs you already own.** Leaves and berries are byproducts of farming and foraging, not dedicated production.
3. **Multiple buyers.** Trademaster, Alchemist and Furniture Maker (from level 3) spread the demand, so one buyer's stock limit does not cap the line.

The realistic view: Paint is a solid early-to-mid income stream, not a late-game engine. Once the [factory](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) and [town orders](/guides/money-guide/) are running, Paint becomes a way to clear leftover green stock rather than the main event.

## How to brew it

1. Study the green carrier you plan to use (Powder variants are pre-known).
2. Put two green carriers in Laboratory I — no need for Laboratory II.
3. Brew. First success records Paint in the Folio.

Skip the Alchemist's finished stock for this one: at 1 bronze to 2 silver a unit, buying Paint to resell is not a business. Buy inputs, brew, sell output.
