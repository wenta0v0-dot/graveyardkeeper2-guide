## The thirteen counters (plan them, don't grind them)

Thirteen achievements are cumulative counters. None of them need dedicated grinding once the automation economy is running — they accumulate in the background:

- **Production counters**: 100 crops (Master Farmer), 100 dishes (Master Cook), 100 bronze ingots (Bronze Age), 100 iron ingots (Iron Age), 100 trees (Tree Bane), 50 bottles of alcohol (Alcohol Baron), 25 potions (Novice Alchemist).
- **Workforce counters**: 50 zombies resurrected (Necromancer), 50 supplies delivered (Supplier) — both run themselves once [the factory](/guides/factory-automation-guide/) is up.
- **Church counters**: 50 sermons (Words of Faith), 50 sermons to a full church (Shepherd of Souls) — the second one just asks you to keep [graveyard quality](/guides/beginner-guide/) high enough to fill the pews.
- **Combat counters**: 50 battles won (Warmaker) — most wins come from the story's scripted battles.
- **Knowledge counter**: 150 inspirations studied (Forbidden Knowledge) — the long pole among the counters; study something every time you pass the table.

## The five hidden achievements — what is actually known

<Callout type="warn" title="Story spoilers below">
  The reports in this section reference the endgame. Stop reading here if you want the story fresh.
</Callout>

Steam ships no condition text for these five, and this page will not invent any. Community database reports (unofficial, verify in game) point at:

- **The Ancient One** — reported as the Bishop's final quest line.
- **An Unlikely Pair** — reported as Davy's quest line.
- **A Dangerous Romance** — reported as Linda's romance arc.
- **Two Broken Souls** — reported as the Red Eye choice in Davy's story (see the [main quest walkthrough](/quests/main-quest-walkthrough/) for why that choice matters).
- **The Other Keeper** — reported as meeting the other Keeper in the Palace.

Treat all five as "late-game story completions" until each one is confirmed in game and this table is updated with a date.
